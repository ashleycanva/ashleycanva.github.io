The challenge

Everything at once

A company-wide rebrand meant a new website, logo and identity, plus 65+ new Color PPF products, all launching together. Dealers still carried the old branding, and the brand was almost invisible to the car owners who actually buy PPF.

01

Execute the rebrand

Rebuilt the brand website on Zoho Sites, created 70 product images for the Color PPF launch, and designed brochures, banners and sales docs for partners.

New website · 70 product assets · launch emails & ads

02

Win over car owners

Shifted ads from trade buyers to consumer pain points ("PPF saves you thousands in paint damage") and made Reels in plain language for everyday drivers.

1 Reel → 20K+ organic views → 3× sales that month

03

Build a system that scales

Set up a marketing funnel, promo calendar, brand protocols and internal docs so every campaign and partner message stays on-brand.

Funnel · promo calendar · brand protocols

The brand came through the rebrand with its partners intact, grew to 400+ national dealers, and gained its first real consumer audience, with plain-language content that tripled sales in a single month.
